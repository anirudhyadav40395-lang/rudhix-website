Founder’s Journey • RUDHIX

The RUDHIX journey started at the age of 16 in Mughalsarai, Subhas Nagar, Uttar Pradesh.

What started as a small idea became an opportunity to learn about customers, products, packaging, pricing, sales and the discipline required to keep improving.

The first step was RUDHIX Kadak Tea — a product created to build experience, understand the market and establish a foundation for the brand.

The goal is not to rush the journey. The goal is to build a strong foundation and gradually explore new consumer categories over time.

The RUDHIX journey is about starting small, learning every day and building step by step.
